Documents Required

What to Bring

📋 Required Documents

  • Birth certificate of the student
  • Transfer certificate (for Grade 2 and above)
  • Recent passport-size photographs (2 copies)
  • Previous year's report card / marksheet
  • Citizenship copy of parent/guardian
  • Medical fitness certificate (for Nursery)
