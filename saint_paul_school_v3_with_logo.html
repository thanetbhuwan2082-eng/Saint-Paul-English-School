About Our School

Nurturing Minds, Shaping Futures

Saint Paul English School is a trusted institution in Kawasoti-17, Gochhada, providing quality English-medium education from Nursery through Grade 10 — preparing students for a bright and successful future.

  • English-medium instruction from Nursery to Grade 10
  • Experienced, caring, and dedicated faculty
  • Holistic development: academics, arts, sports & values
  • Safe, inclusive, and child-friendly environment
  • Active parent-teacher collaboration and community ties

What We Teach

Our Programs & Subjects

English Language & Literacy

Spoken and written English from Nursery onward — building confident communicators at every grade level.

Mathematics

From counting and shapes in early years to algebra and geometry in secondary — strong numeracy throughout.

Science

Hands-on and inquiry-based science — covering environment, biology, physics and chemistry for higher grades.

Social Studies & Nepali

Understanding Nepal's history, geography, culture and civic values alongside Nepali language education.

Arts, Music & Creativity

Encouraging imagination and self-expression through drawing, craft, music and creative activities at all levels.

SEE Board Preparation

Dedicated coaching and model exams for Grade 9 & 10 students to excel in the Secondary Education Examination.
